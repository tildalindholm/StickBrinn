Dagny Sweater

Yarn: Rauma Fivel
Colorway: 300 Naturmelert
Needles: 5mm & 4mm
Gauge: 18sts x 26rows

Written Pattern

Pattern begins here:

collar

Using a 3.5 mm / US 4 needle (30-40 cm / 16" or smaller), CO 104 (104) 104 (112) 112 (112) 120 sts. Join the work, pm for BOR and begin working in the rnd: K 5 rnds to create a rolled neckline. Proceed to separate the work for what will later become the raglan inc’s. You separate already now to distinguish between ribbed and stockinette sts at the neckline. (You may wish to use different m’s to distinguish between them when working the short rows below).

Yoke

Short row 4 from WS: work as sts show in stockinette and rib (remembering to work p sts tbl in rib section) until BOR m, sl BOR m, work rib: p1 tbl, *k1, p1 tbl*, repeat *-* until m7, sl m7, M1Rp, p across left sleeve until m6, M1Lp, sl m6, work rib: p1 tbl, *k1, p1 tbl*, repeat *-* until m5, sl m5, M1Rp, work 3 sts past previous turn, turn

Body

K across back until m1, leave m1 in, work 8 sts rib as they show, CO 3 (5) 7 (9) 11 (13) 15 sts using the backwards loop CO method, place the last 3 rib sts + the 63 (67) 71 (77)81(85)91sleevests+thenext3ribstsonholdon a separate cable or needle (incl m2 and m3), work 8 sts rib, leave in the m, k until next m before rib, leave in m, work 8 sts rib, CO 2 (2) 4 (4) 6 (6) 8 (same method as prev), pm for new BOR, CO 1 (3) 3 (5) 5 (7) 7 sts (same method as prev), place the last 3 rib sts + the 63 (67) 71 (77) 81 (85) 91 sleeve sts + the next 3 rib sts on hold (incl m5 and m6), work 8 sts rib until prev BOR m, which now becomes a regular m to indicate rib.
